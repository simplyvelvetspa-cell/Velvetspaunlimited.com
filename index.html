Meet Victoria Marie Crawley, LMT

Healing is my passion. Empowerment is my purpose.

I’m a passionate massage therapist with more than 16 years of experience dedicated to helping people feel, move, and live better through the healing power of touch.

My career began at the Academy of Somatic Healing Arts and has grown through advanced training in neuromuscular, sports, Swedish, deep-tissue, reflexology, aromatherapy-based wellness, and more than 160 hours of continuing education. I later earned massage-instructor certification through the Ariana Institute and credentials as a Certified Aromatherapist and Advanced Onyxologist.

After eight years serving Atlanta and eight years across Washington, D.C., Maryland, and Virginia, I bring technical mastery, intuitive care, and genuine listening into every Simply Velvet experience. My work is designed to restore balance, relieve pain, improve mobility, and support emotional grounding.

Beyond the treatment room, I founded and lead the Virginia, Washington, D.C., and Maryland Massage Excellence Tournaments & Championships. I competed in the first and second American Massage Championships, placed fourth in Eastern Freestyle Massage, and serve as an official 2026 American Massage Championships judge.

“I educate, empower, and create transformative experiences that restore balance, relieve pain, and promote overall wellness.”
